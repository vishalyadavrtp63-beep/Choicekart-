Frequently Asked Questions

Is ChoiceKart free?

Basic college discovery and database search can be free. Personal counselling plans are paid services.

Does the College Predictor guarantee admission?

No. Predictions are estimates. Final admission depends on official eligibility, cutoff, counselling process and seat availability.

Can I talk to a counsellor?

Yes. Students can select a counselling plan and submit their details.

Which exams are supported?

Currently ChoiceKart includes CUET, NEET, UPCATET and BVSc in this version. More exams can be added through the admin system.

Can I compare colleges?

Yes. Select colleges from predictor results and use the comparison feature.
