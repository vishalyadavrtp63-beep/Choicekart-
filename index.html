❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions students ask.

Is ChoiceKart's college predictor 100% accurate?

No predictor can be 100% accurate. Our predictions are estimates based on past trends and available data — cut-offs change every year based on the number of applicants, seat availability and paper difficulty. Always confirm your options with the official counselling authority before making a final decision.

Is ChoiceKart free to use?

Yes, the college directory, predictor, AI assistant and guides on ChoiceKart are completely free to use.

How do I save colleges to check later?

Tap the 🤍 heart icon on any college card to save it. You can view all your saved colleges anytime from the "❤️ Saved" link in the menu — they're stored right on your device.

Can I compare multiple colleges at once?

Yes — check the "Add to Compare" box on up to 4 college cards, then tap the "Compare Now" bar that appears to see them side by side.

How do I get advice from an expert?

Scroll to the "Advice from Expert" section, fill in your details, and our team will get in touch with you to guide you through the admission process.

I found wrong or outdated information about a college. What do I do?

Please reach out via the Contact Us details in the footer to report it — we'll look into it. College data comes from a public dataset, so occasional inaccuracies are possible.
